## Implications for the next Phase 2 slice

- Preserve three separate assessments: category/activity relevance, personal
  appeal, and current ability to attend. Clear fees, signup and venue information
  support the last assessment.
- Distinguish activity, setting and format: coffee learning, coffee plus dancing,
  food market, performance hall; reserved class versus an informal drop-in
  format where source evidence actually establishes it.
- Distinguish a published repeat schedule from evidence about returning
  participants or community interaction. Do not label an event community-building
  solely because it happens weekly or uses that phrase in marketing.
- Carry the owner's interest in market-style settings and openness to unusual
  coffee/social events as supported development observations. Do not infer
  dislike of noise, classes, religious venues or novelty.
- Source pilot should verify the exact occurrence and navigation/RSVP link,
  preserving URL changes and failures without renewing unsupported freshness.
- Keep the proposed tags / structured attributes / supported summary comparison
  bounded and evaluate on separate examples after any tuning. This review is
  evidence for designing it, not proof that an LLM summary improves ranking.

Immediate technical ordering remains source-discovery/verification first,
then the proposed preference-representation comparison. The owner's delivery
